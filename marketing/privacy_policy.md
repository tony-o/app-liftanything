# Privacy Policy for LiftAnything

**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

LiftAnything ("we", "our", "the app") is a social workout-tracking app. This
policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices
around it.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you register, we collect your name, email
address, and a password. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is
hashed using bcrypt before it touches our database, and we cannot see or
recover your actual password.

**Workout data.** Any workout sessions, exercises, sets, reps, and weights
you log, along with any workout templates and custom exercises you create.

**Social activity.** Kudos (likes) you give or receive on workout sessions,
and the workout sessions of other users you follow/see in your feed.

**Settings.** Your unit preference (imperial/metric), display preferences,
and workout sort order.

**Authentication data.** A session token is issued when you log in, used to
keep you signed in. This token is stored on your device and sent with your
requests; it is not a tracking cookie and is not used for advertising.

## What We Don't Collect

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do
not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, camera/microphone
data, or browsing history. We do not sell or share your personal information
with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

- To create and maintain your account
- To let you log, view, and edit your own workout history
- To show you a social feed of workout activity from other users on the
  service (name, workout name/category, and summary stats — never your
  password or email)
- To remember your app preferences (units, sort order, etc.)

## Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database operated by us. Passwords are
hashed with bcrypt. Access to the database is restricted to the operators
of the service. While we take reasonable measures to protect your
information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and workout data for as long as your account is
active. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact
us at **dinomaniac42@gmail.com** and we will delete it within a reasonable
time.

## Children's Privacy

LiftAnything is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child
has provided us with personal information, contact us at the email above and
we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to
**dinomaniac42@gmail.com**.
