LiftAnything is a social gym app built for lifters who want to track their training and stay motivated with the people they train alongside.

TRACK EVERY SESSION, YOUR WAY
Log any workout — planned or freeform. Skip exercises, do them out of order, or add extras mid-session. Every set tracks weight and reps (or duration, for time-based work like planks and cardio), and LiftAnything automatically shows you what you lifted last time on the same exercise so you always know what to beat.

BUILD YOUR OWN WORKOUTS
Save your go-to routines as reusable workout templates, organized by category — Push, Pull, Legs, Core, or whatever split you follow. Start a session from a template in one tap, or start empty and build it as you go.

A REAL EXERCISE LIBRARY
Pick from a built-in library of common lifts, or add your own custom exercises with custom categories. Mark cardio and holds as time-based so your stats stay accurate.

STAY MOTIVATED TOGETHER
See what your friends are lifting on the Motivation feed, drop kudos on a great session, and keep a running log of your own history on your personal timeline — total volume and session counts included, today and for the week.

MADE FOR HOW YOU ACTUALLY TRAIN
- Imperial or metric units, your choice
- Alphabetical or most-recent sort for your workouts
- Dark mode
- No fluff, no ads interrupting your workout — just you, your lifts, and your people

Whether you're chasing a new PR or just trying to keep showing up, LiftAnything keeps your training organized and your friends in the loop.
